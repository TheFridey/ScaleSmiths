# Brag Plan: ScaleSmiths

## Planning rubric
1. ScaleSmiths designs and develops websites, platforms and connected business systems.
2. Strongest source line: Forge your digital edge.
3. Visual hook: the real forge image, gold #e8a045 and monumental Syne type.
4. Show the public homepage hero, then the actual three engagement routes.
5. 20 seconds is enough for hook, site, capabilities and enquiry CTA.
6. Polished; a confident forge-inspired brand film, with restrained motion and readable holds.
7. Warm music bed at 0.3 with three quiet motion accents.
8. ScaleSmiths. Digital experiences. Intelligent systems. Measurable growth. Discuss your next project at scalesmiths.co.uk.
9. Public marketing journey: hero → Explore Our Capabilities → three engagement routes → Discuss a Project. No private portal or admin content.

## Format and identity
Landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds. No narration.
Background #0b0a08; primary text #f5efe6; secondary #c9bdb0; accent #e8a045.
Display: the repository's self-hosted Syne; body: Arial, matching the site's system sans approach.
LOOK: 1920×1080, 120px safe margins, Syne 700/800, 130px hook, 64px route titles, 2px gold rails; real forge WebP as material.
BANNED: invented performance statistics, prices, testimonials, internal screens, generic particles and visualiser bars.

1. Forge the edge, 0–3.27 (3.27s): real forge art, ScaleSmiths wordmark, FORGE YOUR / DIGITAL EDGE. Text resolves in under 0.5s and holds. Quiet impact on arrival.

Durations sum to 20s. Movement entrances 0.35–0.65s; titles settle and hold.
