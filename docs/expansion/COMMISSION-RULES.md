# Commission rules

All authoritative amounts are integer pence. Versioned contract terms are snapshotted at approval and immutable after acceptance. Standard illustrative rates are 3000 basis points on website revenue and the first Growth Partnership payment: 100000 -> 30000; 100000 -> 30000; 35000 -> 10500; total 70500. Later retainers have no entitlement in the initial plan.

Only authorised manual verification of qualifying VAT-exclusive collected revenue can earn commission. Each collection has an immutable external reference, invoice link, verifier and evidence reference. Partial payments use cumulative rounding so splitting payments cannot increase entitlement. Over-collection, duplicate references with different content, invalid states and over-refunds must fail closed. Identical event replay returns the original result without a second ledger entry.

Custom pricing/rates require explicit founder approval; no partner can edit accepted contract terms or create financial events. Pending contractual maximum is conditional, never earned. Refunds append approved reversals with reasons under the final reviewed agreement; no automatic recovery. Payout preparation locks eligible ledger entries, binds an invoice/reference, requires manual approval and records reconciliation. Nothing in this implementation authorises transfers or self-billing.

Business/legal decisions still required: partner VAT treatment, applicable refund entitlement, invoice requirements, dispute resolution, lead protection term, applicant retention and agreement language. These are release prerequisites, not automated legal conclusions.
