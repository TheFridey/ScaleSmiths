# Partner Academy curriculum

**Status: DRAFT. No course is approved, published or available.** Initial lesson content is in [LESSONS-FOUNDATIONS.md](LESSONS-FOUNDATIONS.md) (courses 1 to 6) and [LESSONS-PRACTICE.md](LESSONS-PRACTICE.md) (courses 7 to 13).

## Principles

- **Optional and voluntary.** The programme describes the Academy as optional and voluntary (`FEATURE_AVAILABILITY` in `web/src/lib/partner-programme.ts`). There are no compulsory courses, deadlines, scores, certificates, rankings or completion targets, and completion never affects commission, allocation, recognition or standing. Wording is "you may find it useful", never "you must". Solicitor review: `AP-GOV-02`.
- **Approved sources only.** Each lesson lists its sources. Where a source is a draft or a proposal, the lesson says so in the text.
- **Teach the boundary, not just the pitch.** Each service course has a lesson on what the service is *not*, and when to escalate.
- **Short lessons.** Five to ten minutes each, readable on a phone.
- **Knowledge checks are optional self-checks.** Answers are shown immediately and nothing is stored except "attempted".

## C01 Welcome to ScaleSmiths

**Purpose:** a clear, honest picture of who ScaleSmiths is, what it sells and how the partnership works.

**Learning objectives.** After this course a partner can:
1. describe ScaleSmiths and its services in two sentences without overstating them;
2. explain what an independent partner does and does not do;
3. find approved answers in the Resource Centre and know when to ask for help;
4. tell the difference between approved, proposed and planned features.
