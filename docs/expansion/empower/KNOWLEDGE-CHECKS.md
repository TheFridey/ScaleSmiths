## C01 Welcome to ScaleSmiths

- [x] a: Website design and development from £2,000

### C01-L2 q2 true_false
A custom platform can be priced against the website starting price.

### C02-L8 q1 single

## C03 SEO and Digital Growth

### C03-L4 q1 single
Can anyone guarantee first place on Google?
- [ ] a: Yes, with enough budget
- [x] b: No, because rankings depend on Google's systems, competitors and the searcher
- [ ] c: Yes, if the site is built by ScaleSmiths
- [ ] d: Only for local searches
> Explanation: No one can guarantee a position. ScaleSmiths commits to the work and to measuring progress against a recorded baseline.

### C03-L4 q2 true_false
Technical fixes can change indexation quickly, while content and authority work is measured over months.
- answer: true
> Explanation: That is the honest timescale: quick technical changes, slower gains from content and authority.

### C03-L5 q1 single
Is ongoing SEO included in every ScaleSmiths build?
- [x] a: No. Search foundations are part of every build; ongoing SEO is separate, as a project or through a Growth Partnership
- [ ] b: Yes, ongoing SEO is always included
- [ ] c: Only for sites over ten pages

### C03-L6 q1 true_false
ScaleSmiths will say when paid search is the more honest answer for a client.
- answer: true
> Explanation: Ads buy visibility immediately and stop with the budget; SEO compounds slowly. The right mix depends on urgency, margin and competition.

### C03-L7 q1 multiple
Which of these must you not say about AI search?
- [x] a: ScaleSmiths can get your business into ChatGPT
- [x] b: ScaleSmiths can guarantee your business is cited by AI tools
- [ ] c: Clear, well-structured answers help AI tools understand a business
- [x] d: ScaleSmiths controls what AI systems say about you
> Explanation: AI systems are run by third parties and change often. Nobody can guarantee inclusion or control what they say; well-structured, accurate content gives them good material to work from.

### C03-L8 q1 single
Where does diagnosing "we're not on Google" usually start?
- [x] a: Google Search Console
- [ ] b: Buying ads
- [ ] c: Rebuilding the site straight away
- [ ] d: Asking the client's competitors

## C05 Website Migration

### C05-L3 q1 single
Can the search risk of a rebuild be removed completely?
- [ ] a: Yes, with redirects
- [x] b: No. It can be reduced, but nobody can honestly guarantee rankings
- [ ] c: Yes, if the domain stays the same
- [ ] d: Only for small sites
> Explanation: Any rebuild carries some search risk. Careful mapping and checking reduce it; nothing removes it.

### C05-L3 q2 multiple
Which of these reduce search risk in a migration?
- [x] a: Mapping old URLs to new ones with redirects
- [x] b: Carrying over content that performs
- [x] c: Preserving metadata and structured data
- [ ] d: Deleting old pages without redirects
> Explanation: URL mapping, keeping performing content, preserving metadata and checking indexing after launch all reduce risk. Removing pages without redirects increases it.

### C05-L4 q1 multiple
Which of these should you collect before a migration is scoped?
- [x] a: Who controls the domain and registrar
- [x] b: Search Console and analytics access, if any
- [ ] c: The client's registrar password
- [x] d: A list of integrations such as forms, booking and payments
> Explanation: Collect who controls what, access arrangements, integrations, licences and deadlines. Never ask for passwords.

### C05-L5 q1 true_false
A red flag such as a domain close to expiry is a reason to walk away from the opportunity.
- answer: false
> Explanation: None of the red flags is a reason to walk away, but every one is a reason to escalate early.

### C05-L6 q1 single
When does a migration go to ScaleSmiths for technical scoping?
- [x] a: Always, before any price or timeline is discussed
- [ ] b: Only if the client asks
- [ ] c: After you have agreed a price
- [ ] d: Only for e-commerce sites
> Explanation: Every migration goes to technical scoping first, with what you collected.
