## ScaleSmiths

**Websites and systems built around how your business actually wins customers.**

ScaleSmiths is a web and software studio based in Hucknall, Nottinghamshire, working with businesses across the UK. We build custom websites, platforms and business systems, and we look after them afterwards.

### How we work

- **We start with what already works.** Rankings, enquiries and systems that perform are protected, not thrown away.
- **We write the scope down.** Every proposal lists what is included, what is not and the assumptions behind the schedule.
- **We deliver in phases.** A dependable first release, then improvements that follow evidence.
- **We do not promise what nobody controls.** No guaranteed rankings or results; clear commitments to the work and to measuring it.

### What we do

| Service | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Website design and development | From £2,000 |

| Custom platforms, business systems and larger transformations | Scoped after discovery |

A starting price is the entry point for an agreed scope. It is not unlimited development, marketing, support, integrations or a guaranteed result. Every proposal depends on requirements, complexity and the deliverables we write down. Prices are in GBP. We do not bill in other currencies or publish fixed converted prices.
