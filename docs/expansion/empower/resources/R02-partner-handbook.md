## 1. Welcome

This handbook explains how the ScaleSmiths partnership works day to day. It is a practical guide, not a contract.

## 2. Your relationship with ScaleSmiths

*[PROPOSED POLICY, solicitor review]* You are an independent partner, not an employee. There is no salary, guaranteed income, compulsory working hours or sales target. You decide whether, when and how you work, and whether to use the Partner Academy. Commission is gross, before your own expenses and tax.

## 3. What you do and what ScaleSmiths does

| You | ScaleSmiths |
|---|---|
| Identify and introduce suitable businesses | Reviews every opportunity |
| Hold early, honest conversations | Scopes, prices and writes proposals |
| Register prospects in the portal | Contracts with and invoices the client |
| Support the relationship | Delivers and supports the work |
| Pass on questions | Answers technical, commercial and legal questions |

## 4. The Partner Portal

Sign in on the ScaleSmiths admin site. The portal holds your prospects and scope requests, approved resources, commission records, onboarding toolkit and meeting requests. Use it as the single place for opportunity information.

## 5. Approved content

Use only approved, published content. Each item shows its version, approval date and review date, and whether it is **client-facing** or **partner only**. If an answer is not in the portal, ask; do not improvise.

## 6. Registering prospects

Register before detailed conversations. Duplicates and existing clients are checked. *[FOUNDER DECISION `AP-COM-06`: lead protection start, expiry, reassignment and disputes.]*

## 7. Contacting businesses

*[BLOCKED `AP-LEG-03`: approved marketing process.]* Until the process is approved, work through your own network and introductions, and do not make unsolicited calls or send unsolicited marketing emails on ScaleSmiths' behalf.

## 8. Getting help

Request a meeting in the portal: joint sales, technical scoping, bespoke discovery, commercial assistance, proposal review, training or escalation. See [R10 Technical escalation guide](R10-technical-escalation-guide.md).

## 9. Commission

See [R12 Commission and payment reference](R12-commission-and-payment-reference.md). Everything there is proposed until the agreement and terms are approved.

## 10. Conduct

Be truthful, never pressure anyone, protect personal data, keep confidential information private, and tell ScaleSmiths quickly about complaints or mistakes. The Professional Conduct course (C12) expands on this.

## 11. Personal data

*[Privacy adviser review `AP-PRIV-01`]* Collect only what you need; store it in the portal; never ask clients for passwords; respect every request not to be contacted.
