## Your new website, explained

### What a project includes

Agreed messaging, page structure, a responsive build that works well on phones, search foundations (metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, headings, internal links, indexation controls and page speed) and a clear route from visitor to enquiry, with launch support for that scope. Your proposal is the definitive list.

### What is separate unless your proposal includes it

Hosting, ongoing SEO, content programmes, integrations and open-ended support. Managed business email is also separate unless combined in writing.

### Price

Website design and development starts from £2,000. That is a starting point for an agreed scope, not unlimited design, content, integrations, marketing or support. Your quote is based on your requirements, the complexity of the journeys and systems, and the deliverables we write down.

### Timing

We confirm timing after discovery. Content readiness, integrations, review cycles and technical risk affect the schedule more than the number of pages. The proposal records the delivery range and its assumptions.

### What we need from you

Information about what you sell and who buys it; what your current site does well; any evidence you have (analytics, Search Console, enquiry records); content, images and brand assets; and access to your domain, hosting and any connected systems. Projects move fastest when these are identified early.

### Ownership

Ownership of the bespoke work is set out in the signed project documents and transfers once the agreed fees are paid. You keep your own materials, trademarks and business data.

### Editing your site

If you expect to change content often, we can build an editable content layer. Tell us what you expect to change and how often, and the proposal will say exactly what is editable.

### Moving from your current site

We review what the current site already does well (pages that rank, enquiries, integrations and hosting) before recommending a rebuild, a focused repair or a migration. When we migrate, we map existing URLs to their new equivalents with redirects, carry over content that performs, preserve metadata and check indexing after launch. Any rebuild carries some search risk, and nobody can honestly guarantee rankings.

### Hosting

Hosting can be arranged as part of a build or as a managed service. We choose it around the workload and support model, and document who is accountable.
