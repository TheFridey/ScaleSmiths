## The Growth Partnership

An ongoing partnership that keeps improving your website and your visibility after launch, whether or not ScaleSmiths built your current site.

### What it can include

Depending on the agreed scope: technical SEO and search visibility, service and location content, conversion improvements, analytics and measurement, automation, hosting, monitoring, updates, fixes and roadmap delivery.

### What it is not

It is not an unlimited marketing package or a promise of results. Maintenance keeps your site dependable; SEO, content, design changes and new integrations are improvement work, prioritised on your roadmap.

### Price

From £350 per month. Your proposal defines priorities, working cadence, responsibilities and commercial terms.

### How work is prioritised

We work to an agreed roadmap and a prioritised backlog rather than a first-come queue, so commercially important work is not displaced by whatever arrived last. Incidents affecting a live service come first. You can raise and track requests in the ScaleSmiths client portal.

### If something breaks

Under an agreed arrangement, the escalation route, contacts and cadence are documented, and ScaleSmiths can carry out emergency maintenance and proportionate protective action. Response commitments exist only where they have been contracted.

### If we did not build your site

We can often take it on after a review of its code, hosting, access and update path. That review may identify repairs needed first. We will not take responsibility for an unknown system blind.
