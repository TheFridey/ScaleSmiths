## Being found: search and AI search

### What SEO is

Search engine optimisation is the work that makes a website findable, understandable and worth choosing in search results: technical foundations, content that answers real searches, and an on-page experience that turns a click into an enquiry. It is ongoing work.

### Local SEO

For people searching for a provider in a place: clear service and area pages, consistent business details across the web, a Google Business Profile that supports the site, and visible local proof.

### Technical SEO

Crawlability, canonical URLs, sitemaps, redirects, structured data, heading hierarchy, mobile rendering, Core Web Vitals and HTTPS. Content cannot perform if the technical layer works against it.

### AI search and answer engine optimisation (AEO)

*[DRAFT: new wording, founder approval required]* More people now ask AI assistants and AI-generated search summaries for recommendations. Those systems work best with clear, accurate, well-structured information. We build pages that answer buyers' real questions directly, keep facts consistent, and use structured data and FAQ content. Nobody can control or guarantee what an AI system says about a business, because those systems are run by third parties and change often.

### What to expect

Technical fixes can change indexation quickly; content and authority work is measured over months. No one can guarantee first place on Google. We commit to the work, and to measuring it against a recorded baseline.

### In a new build, and afterwards

Search foundations are part of every ScaleSmiths build. Ongoing SEO is separate: a project, or part of a Growth Partnership.

### Paid search

Ads buy visibility immediately and stop when the budget does; SEO compounds slowly. We will tell you when paid search is the more honest answer for your timeframe.
