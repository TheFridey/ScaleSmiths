# R07 Discovery questionnaire

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `DRAFT` |
| Audience | Partner only (the notes are shared with ScaleSmiths, not the client) |
| Review owner | Both founders; privacy adviser for data fields |
| Sources | faq: project-inputs, after-enquiry; Academy C08 |
| Review questions | Are any fields unnecessary under data minimisation (`AP-PRIV-01`)? Should partners ask about budget? |

Record facts the client told you separately from your own observations. Do not record sensitive personal data. Do not record passwords.

## A. The business

1. Business name, website address and main location.
2. What do you sell? Which services matter most commercially?
3. Who are your best customers? Which areas do you serve?
4. How do new customers usually find you today?

## B. The current website and presence

5. What does the current website do well?
6. What frustrates you about it?
7. Who built it, and who looks after it now?
8. Who controls the domain, hosting and website accounts? *(Name or role only; never ask for passwords.)*
9. Do you have Google Search Console, analytics or a Google Business Profile? Can ScaleSmiths be given access later if needed?

## C. Evidence

10. Roughly how many enquiries or calls come from the website?
11. Which pages or searches bring customers now, if known?

## D. Systems

12. What tools do you use for booking, quotes, customer records, email, payments or reporting?
13. Where are things done manually or in spreadsheets?
14. Do your business emails ever go to spam? What email address do you use?

## E. Goals and constraints

15. What would a good result look like in twelve months?
16. Is there a deadline or trigger (a launch, a hosting contract ending, a new service)?
17. Who else is involved in the decision?
18. Have you set aside a budget range? *(Record only if offered. Do not quote.)*
