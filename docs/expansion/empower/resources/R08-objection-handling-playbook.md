# R08 Objection-handling playbook

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `DRAFT`; each entry needs individual approval |
| Audience | Partner only, with client-facing lines marked |
| Review owner | Both founders |
| Sources | faq library (keys cited per entry), `published-starting-prices.ts`, [DISCOUNT-ALLOWANCES.md](../../intelligence/DISCOUNT-ALLOWANCES.md) |

Method: **acknowledge, clarify, answer, confirm.** Never resolve an objection with a discount, a guarantee, a timeline or a price that is not published.

| ID | Objection | Clarifying question | Partner guidance | Client-facing line | Escalate when | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `AP-OBJ-01` | "It's too expensive." | "Compared with what, and for what scope?" | Explain the starting-price boundary, phasing and the audit. Never discount; allowances are disabled and founder-only *[PROPOSED POLICY]* | "The starting price is an entry point for an agreed scope. A proposal lists exactly what's included, and work can be phased. If you'd like a lower-cost first step, the Business Growth Audit is £395." | The client asks for a specific discount or payment plan → commercial assistance | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-02` | "We already have an agency." | "What's working well with them?" | Respect the relationship. Offer a second opinion only | "That's fine. ScaleSmiths always starts by looking at what already works. An audit gives you an independent view, and the findings are useful whoever does the work." | Client complains about the current supplier → do not engage; escalation | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-03` | "Can you guarantee page one?" (faq:ranking-guarantees) | "What would page one mean for the business?" | Never promise rankings, traffic or revenue | "No honest agency can promise a position, because rankings depend on Google, competitors and the searcher. What ScaleSmiths commits to is the work, and measuring progress against a baseline." | Never | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-04` | "We use WordPress / Wix / Shopify." (faq:wordpress, faq:ecommerce-builds) | "What do you like about it?" | Not anti-platform | "ScaleSmiths looks at what your current platform does well before recommending whether a rebuild is justified. Sometimes improving what you have is the better decision." | Migration discussed → technical scoping | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-05` | "How long will it take?" (faq:timeline) | "Is there a date you're working towards?" | Never give a timeline | "Timing is confirmed after discovery, because content readiness and integrations affect it more than page count. The proposal records the range and the assumptions." | Hard deadline → joint sales or technical scoping | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-06` | "Will we lose our rankings?" (faq:seo-rebuild, *owner review*) | "Which pages bring you customers today?" | Acknowledge risk honestly | "Any rebuild carries some risk, and nobody can guarantee rankings. ScaleSmiths reduces it by mapping old pages to new ones, keeping content that performs and checking indexing after launch." | Always for migrations → technical scoping | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-07` | "We need to think about it." | "What would help you decide?" | Respect it; summarise; follow up once on an agreed date | "Of course. I'll send a short summary of what we discussed, and check back on [date] if that's alright." | Never | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-08` | "Who owns the website?" (faq:website-ownership) | — | Use published wording only | "Ownership is set out in the signed project documents and transfers once the agreed fees are paid. You keep your own content, trademarks and data." | Specific contract question → commercial assistance | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-09` | "Can't you just do it cheaper with AI?" | "What would you want it to do?" | Do not disparage AI or oversell it | "AI tools can help with some tasks, but a site still needs accurate content, solid foundations and someone accountable for it. ScaleSmiths uses automation where it genuinely helps." | Never | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-10` | "Are you employed by ScaleSmiths?" | — | Be accurate about independence | "I'm an independent partner of ScaleSmiths. ScaleSmiths scopes, contracts and delivers the work directly." | Never | `DRAFT`, `BLOCKED` (`AP-GOV-05` title) |
| `AP-OBJ-11` | "Do you do hosting / email too?" (faq:hosting-provided, faq:business-email) | "What do you use now?" | Published facts only | "Yes. Hosting can be part of a build or a managed service, and Managed Business Email starts from £15 a month for three mailboxes, with setup included." | DNS or email delivery problems now → technical scoping | `DRAFT` |
| `AP-OBJ-12` | "We're not ready yet." | "What would need to change?" | Accept it; offer a resource; no pressure | "No problem. I can send you a short guide, and you're welcome to get in touch when the time is right." | Never | `DRAFT` |

## Limitations and supporting resources

Each response must be read with its limitation. Added in the B1 content review ([../CONTENT-REVIEW-REGISTER.md](../CONTENT-REVIEW-REGISTER.md)).

| ID | Limitation (what the response must not be stretched to mean) | Supporting approved resource or entry |
|---|---|---|
| `AP-OBJ-01` | No discount, payment plan or "special price" may be offered or hinted at; the audit price is fixed at £395 | R11, KB-TERMS-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-02` | Never criticise the current supplier or ask for their pricing; a second opinion is not a promise that ScaleSmiths will find problems | R03, KB-SEO-04 |
| `AP-OBJ-03` | No position, traffic, enquiry or revenue figure, including "typical" results | R06, KB-SEO-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-04` | Not a promise that the current platform can be kept, or that a migration is advisable | R03, KB-MIG-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-05` | No range, "usually", or week count, even informally | R03 |
| `AP-OBJ-06` | Risk is reduced, never removed; no "no ranking loss" promise | R03, KB-RED-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-07` | One agreed follow-up, then stop if there is no reply (R13 T4, T5) | R13 |
| `AP-OBJ-08` | Do not interpret contract clauses; ownership follows the signed documents | KB-TERMS-02 |
| `AP-OBJ-09` | Do not claim AI is or is not used on a particular project; do not compare prices with AI tools | KB-AUTO-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-10` | Do not describe commission or partner terms to the client | R02 |
| `AP-OBJ-11` | No hosting price, uptime figure or deliverability guarantee | KB-HOST-01, KB-EMAIL-01 |
| `AP-OBJ-12` | Send only an approved client-facing resource; no repeat contact without agreement | R01, R13 |

No response uses case studies, testimonials, statistics or competitor comparisons. Any future proof point needs `AP-PROOF-01`.
