# R09 Meeting preparation checklist

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `DRAFT` |
| Audience | Partner only |
| Review owner | Both founders |
| Sources | Academy C08 |

## Before the meeting

- [ ] The prospect is registered in the Partner Portal, and the portal does not show it as awaiting commercial approval from a founder.
- [ ] I have looked at their website on a phone and on a computer.
- [ ] I searched for their main service and town, and noted whether they appear.
- [ ] I checked whether they have a Google Business Profile and read recent reviews.
- [ ] I noted questions, not conclusions.
- [ ] I have the discovery questionnaire (R07) ready.
- [ ] I have checked the current versions of any resources I plan to share (version and review date shown).
- [ ] I know which published starting price, if any, is relevant.
- [ ] I have confirmed the time, place or link, and how long they have.

## During

- [ ] Purpose and agenda agreed at the start.
- [ ] I listened more than I spoke.
- [ ] I quoted only published starting prices, with the boundary.
- [ ] I gave no timeline, guarantee, discount or contract term.
- [ ] I was clear that I am an independent partner.
- [ ] One clear next step agreed.

## After (same day)

- [ ] Notes written in the questionnaire format, facts separated from observations.
- [ ] Portal record updated.
- [ ] Follow-up sent using an approved template (R13).
- [ ] Escalation or meeting request raised if needed.
- [ ] Any "do not contact" request recorded and passed on.
