# R13 Professional follow-up templates

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `DRAFT`; each template needs approval |
| Audience | Client-facing (sent by the partner to a prospect who has agreed to be contacted) |
| Review owner | Both founders; privacy adviser (`AP-PRIV-01`, `AP-LEG-03`) |
| Review questions | Approved sign-off and title (`AP-GOV-05`)? Must templates include an opt-out line? Approved channels? |

Rules: send only to people you have spoken to who agreed to hear from you; personalise every message; no pressure or false urgency; include only published prices; stop if asked.

*[REQUIRES LEGAL AND PRIVACY REVIEW]* Whether T6 (a single acknowledgement after an opt-out) should be sent at all, through which channel, and whether every template needs an opt-out line, are open under `AP-LEG-03` and `AP-PRIV-01`. Until decided, record the opt-out in the portal and do not send T6 without ScaleSmiths' agreement.

## T1 After a discovery conversation

> Subject: Our conversation about [business name]
>
> Hello [name],
>
> Thank you for your time today. To summarise what you told me:
>
> - [point 1]
> - [point 2]
>
> The next step we agreed was [next step]. [If a quote: I'll ask ScaleSmiths to prepare a proper quote, and they will be in touch directly.]
>
> I'll check back on [date] unless you'd prefer otherwise.
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]
> Independent partner of ScaleSmiths *[title pending `AP-GOV-05`]*

## T3 Introducing ScaleSmiths for a joint meeting

> Hello [name],
>
> As discussed, I'd like to introduce you to ScaleSmiths so they can go through [topic] in more detail. Could you let me know two or three times that suit you over the next couple of weeks?
>
> Kind regards, [name]

## T4 After "we need to think about it"

> Hello [name],
>
> Thanks again for considering it. I've attached a short guide [approved resource] in case it helps. I'll check in on [date]; if the timing isn't right, just let me know and I won't chase.
>
> Kind regards, [name]

## T5 Closing the loop respectfully

> Hello [name],
>
> I haven't heard back, so I'll assume now isn't the right time. I won't contact you again about this, but you're very welcome to get in touch whenever it would be useful.
>
> Kind regards, [name]

## T6 Acknowledging a request not to be contacted

> Hello [name],
>
> Understood. I've noted that you'd prefer not to be contacted, and I've passed this on to ScaleSmiths. Apologies for any inconvenience.
>
> Kind regards, [name]
