# Independent Phase A.1 review

The financial and database reviewer and the security reviewer inspected the foundation independently. Operational review ran separately and used synthetic fixtures. Source findings below describe the original checkpoint. Verification status is recorded in the final report.

| ID | Severity | Source and original behaviour | Expected behaviour, impact and disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIN-01 | HIGH | `admin/src/lib/server/expansion-finance.ts` always posted a refund adjustment from a free-text approval reference. | Cash, entitlement and recovery must be distinct. Added manual review, an immutable clause check for unpaid adjustments, a preparation/approval hold and retained-entitlement resolution. Paid recovery remains an explicit business decision. |
| FIN-02 | HIGH | `0064_command_centre.sql` and `0069_expansion_finance.sql` allow one reversal. | Cash can be refunded in parts without breaking canonical reporting. Forward migration bounds linked net and VAT and preserves ordinary full-only reversals. Verify split refunds, running limits, concurrent over-refunds and legacy history. |
| FIN-03 | HIGH | `0069_expansion_finance.sql` gives a prepared batch permanent exclusive allocation. | Cancel a still-prepared batch without rewriting the batch or allocations. Migration backfills exclusive claims and releases them only through cancellation. Verify approve/cancel races and reallocation. |
| FIN-04 | HIGH | Collection verification accepts any issued invoice for the same client. | New commission collection requires a reviewed, immutable invoice/contract/milestone allocation with evidence and net limit. The founder still verifies the signed contract and invoice line basis. Exact historical replay survives the upgrade. |
| FIN-05 | HIGH | Expansion cancellation originally left the linked canonical contract confirmed. The generic CRM can also end a contract. | Expansion cancellation ends the linked commercial contract. New collections check its canonical confirmed state. Exact historical replay is preserved. Bespoke acceptance requires separately declared signed pricing and rejects qualifying caps above signed prices. |
| FIN-06 | MEDIUM | A payout could be prepared without checking its invoice amount. Case variants could duplicate settlement references. | Preparation compares the reviewed invoice net amount to exact ledger pence. Case-insensitive settlement reuse is checked under a transaction lock. Bank verification and VAT treatment are separate operational controls. |
| FIN-07 | MEDIUM | Separate founder finance reads were made at read committed. | Finance snapshot now reads from one repeatable-read transaction. Partner finance already used repeatable read, so the original hypothesis of a partner snapshot defect was dismissed. |
| SEC-01 | HIGH | `partnerSnapshot` returned published client resources to all partners. | Project only published partner resources. Regression includes an approved client-only resource. |
| SEC-02 | MEDIUM | Internal feasibility/pricing review notes were returned in the partner projection. | Founder-only notes stay in founder administration. Partner-visible next steps need an explicitly approved projection. |
| SEC-03 | HIGH | Closing recruitment did not close invitation issuance/consumption. | Added explicit default-closed activation gate for both code paths. Existing activated sessions retain their normal authority boundaries. |
| SEC-04 | MEDIUM | Web DB role could insert founder status and confirmation columns even though the route filtered them. | Restrict grants to permitted intake columns. The live grant harness verifies permitted insertion and rejects protected fields, including injected drift. |
| SEC-05 | MEDIUM | Registration and suppression could race without a shared lock. | Expansion's suppression operation now takes the registration lock. The generic contact registry and operational screening remain separate. |
| OPS-01 | HIGH | Expired unlinked applications were deleted irrespective of active lifecycle. | Preview only eligible terminal records, respect holds and linked profiles, recheck under transaction, require explicit execution configuration and actor authority. |
| OPS-02 | MEDIUM | A successfully committed command could look like a failure when the follow-up refresh failed. | Separate mutation outcome and refresh error. Interrupted requests report the uncertain outcome and prompt reconciliation. |
| OPS-03 | HIGH | Isolated populated restore exposed sequence ownership transfer before the owning table. | Existing regulatory and migration roles need to be reapplied safely after restore. See isolated backup/restore evidence for disposition. |

Test reproducers exercise these outcomes in the owning test suites or the isolated grant and restore harness. See SECURITY-VERIFICATION.md for the access-boundary details and REFUND-AND-ADJUSTMENT-MODEL.md for the financial disposition.
