# Manual payout lifecycle

Preparation compares the partner invoice net amount against the sum of available signed ledger entries. Previously consumed entries remain claimed. Review past and prepared liabilities separately. Use a distinct corrected invoice reference for a replacement. Exact repeated preparation returns the prior record.

Only prepared payouts can be cancelled. The founder records a reason and confirms that no external payment was sent or scheduled. An immutable cancellation record releases the exclusive claims. The original batch, total, preparer and allocation history remain visible. No replacement is issued automatically.

Approval confirms the prepared batch and is blocked by unresolved refund review. Only an approved batch can record an external settlement reference. The reference is unique across batches, including case variants. The displayed paid amount is a founder-recorded settlement. Maintain independently checked bank evidence in the approved accounting process.

Partner invoice VAT treatment, disputes, recovery after settlement and approved-but-uncertain payments require accounting instructions. No bank credentials, bank transfer or self-billing feature is part of this workflow.
