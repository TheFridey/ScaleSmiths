# Refunds and adjustments

Record verified cash refunds from the original collection. Enter integer net and gross refunded pence. Omitting both takes the remaining balance. An explicitly entered net without gross records a net-only refund. The final refund must reconcile both remaining components. Refund evidence records the actual client refund and its unique reference.

Cash and commission have separate effects. The ledger records what is authorised under the accepted agreement. The mathematical adjustment is evaluated from cumulative signed cash and kept with the refund for review.

| Treatment | Effect |
|---|---|
| Manual review | Records cash and calculated impact. Holds future preparation or approval until reviewed. Existing approved settlement can still be recorded if it actually happened. |
| Retain entitlement | Records the approval evidence and leaves earned commission in place. A pending review can be resolved to retention with append-only evidence. |
| Adjust unpaid | Requires a matching unpaid-adjustment clause reference in the immutable accepted plan. Applies the calculated delta only before that milestone has any live or consumed payout claim. Cancel a prepared batch first when eligible. |

Older agreements with no captured clause default to manual review. Previously paid commission has no automatic debit or future offset. Any decision requiring recovery or a different resolution of a pending refund needs a reviewed operational and engineering process. Ordinary canonical Command Centre payments retain their full-only reversal rule.

After recording a partial refund or reassigning a cancelled allocation, the original foundation application is unsuitable for financial projections. Restore a compatible application or pause affected financial paths and make a forward correction.
