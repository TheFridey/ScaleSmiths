# Personal partner webpages (checkpoint 5, migration 0087)

**Eligibility:** a personal-webpage reward exists for the partner (five qualifying clients under the programme). Approval of wording additionally needs that reward approved under an approved programme version; the database refuses otherwise. If the reward is later withdrawn the public page disappears.

**Workflow:** partner drafts, records consent, submits; a founder approves, requests changes or rejects; approved wording is an immutable version; a founder separately publishes it (a publication is a second immutable record); a founder can supersede or revoke it, and **the partner can withdraw consent and take their own page down at once** (`withdraw_profile_consent`, no founder needed; a final review found this missing).

- **Consent** is bound to the exact wording by a database-computed content hash. Any edit clears it. Submission without matching consent is refused by the database. The consent text (`profile-consent-v2`, which now also names the enquiry reference, founder-approved achievement wording and any granted recognition) is a draft awaiting legal and privacy approval.
- **Slugs** are assigned by a founder, are unique, protected against reserved words, and stay bound to one partner for ever, even after revocation. Another live partner's display name cannot be reused. Display names cannot include ScaleSmiths or authority titles.
- **Content rules:** no email addresses, phone numbers or web addresses; nothing suggesting ownership, directorship, employment or guaranteed results; achievements must not mention money or earnings.
- **Public projection:** the web database role may `SELECT` only the security-barrier view `expansion_public_partner_profiles` (display name, biography, operating area, enquiry mode, opted-in referral identifier, founder-selected achievement wording, founding-recognition date). The view tests liveness on every read: live publication, active partner, entitled reward. The web role is denied every private table (tested as that role).
- **Cache behaviour:** `/partners/[slug]` renders at request time with no stored shell and is never indexed. A removal is visible on the next request (tested in a production-build browser run).
- **Switches (both default off):** `PARTNER_PROFILE_PUBLICATION_ENABLED=true` on the admin app permits publishing; `PUBLIC_PARTNER_PROFILES_ENABLED=true` on the web app serves pages. Real partner pages are a separate release gate that has not been opened.
- **Known limits (independent reviews, low severity):** achievement wording is founder-typed static text, so revoking recognition removes the badge and date but not an achievement line that mentions it (revoke the publication to remove everything); the public view now also requires **current entitlement** (migration 0089): the partner's founder-qualified client count must still meet the reward's milestone and no qualification review flag may be open, so a revoked qualification or a refund under review suspends the page on the next request, with every record kept; restoring entitlement through a founder decision (for example maintaining a flagged qualification) restores the page automatically, because its wording is an already-approved immutable version; re-activating a deactivated partner revives a previously live page without re-review; the referral reference shown on a page is the recruitment-referral identifier; the consent hash covers the wording, not the consent text version; the biography is screened for contact details, authority claims and income promises, with founder review as the final guard.
- **Not built:** partner photographs. There is no secure upload, scanning, storage or serving pipeline, so photographs are unsupported rather than approximated.
