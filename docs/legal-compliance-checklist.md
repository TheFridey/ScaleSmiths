# ScaleSmiths legal and compliance operations checklist

This internal checklist records work that public policy pages cannot make true. It is not legal advice or a release approval. Evidence, owner decisions and qualified UK legal review must be retained outside the public repository where sensitive.

## Identity and commercial decisions

- [ ] Confirm the contracting legal person/entity, entity type, legal name and trading-name authority.
- [ ] If incorporated, confirm company number, registered jurisdiction and registered office disclosure.
- [ ] Confirm VAT registration/status and how the £15 email price is treated.
- [ ] Confirm Managed Business Email billing interval, recurring status, payment date, cancellation effective date and minimum commitment before enabling checkout.
- [ ] Obtain owner and solicitor approval for a commercially appropriate liability cap; none is published by default.
- [ ] Review consumer eligibility and Consumer Contracts/Consumer Rights Act flows before accepting any consumer order.
- [ ] Review the DMCC subscription-contract regime before launch and again before its expected commencement in spring 2027.

## Data protection operations

- [ ] Determine ICO data protection fee applicability and complete registration/payment if required; do not claim registration until verified.
- [ ] Maintain controller records of processing, processor records where required, lawful-basis/legitimate-interest assessments and a real retention schedule.
- [ ] Maintain DSAR identity verification, search, export, correction, restriction, deletion and objection procedures.
- [ ] Maintain a direct-marketing PECR review and suppression/do-not-contact process.
- [ ] Review the privacy notice, cookie inventory and subprocessors after every material provider or feature change and at least periodically.
- [ ] Execute Article 28 terms with clients and processor contracts with subprocessors; assess UK international-transfer safeguards and transfer risk.
- [ ] Complete the external email infrastructure entry: operator, VPS/datacentre, backup provider, processing region, support access and transfer position.
- [ ] Complete production hosting, Cloudflare, Sentry and Resend service/region/contract records.

## Managed email and PECR communications-service duties

- [ ] Obtain qualified advice on whether the offered service is a public electronic communications service under PECR and document the conclusion.
- [ ] If applicable, maintain PECR security measures, security-risk customer communications, traffic-data rules, breach assessment and a personal-data breach log.
- [ ] If applicable, notify the ICO within the current PECR timeframe and notify affected customers where the legal threshold is met; keep the separate procedure current.
- [ ] Maintain abuse intake, spam/reputation monitoring, compromise response, credential reset, rate limiting and proportionate suspension procedures.
- [ ] Maintain secure DNS access exchange; never collect registrar/DNS passwords through public enquiry forms. Prefer delegated/scoped access where supported.
- [ ] Maintain mailbox onboarding/offboarding, authorised administrator, password-reset, export/migration, deletion and backup-rotation procedures.
- [ ] Document what support is included and how complex migrations or unrelated IT work are approved and charged.

## Security, incident and resilience

- [ ] Maintain and rehearse incident response, controller notification, regulatory assessment, evidence preservation and communications plans.
- [ ] Maintain breach notification decision records; do not automatically claim every incident is reported within 72 hours.
- [ ] Test encrypted backups and isolated restores; document retention, access and deletion through rotation.
- [ ] Maintain vulnerability intake and responsible-disclosure triage.
- [ ] Review CSP, security headers, session cookie options, access controls, secrets, rate limiting and monitoring redaction after material changes.

## Terms and acceptance records

- [ ] Preserve the exact legal policy versions supplied with each paid order and include durable links/copies in confirmation communications.
- [ ] Record account/order identifier, affirmative acceptance, server timestamp, Service Terms version, service-specific terms version and AUP version for actual purchases.
- [ ] The current email route is an onboarding enquiry, not a payment checkout. Its database creation time and submitted brief record version 2.0 acknowledgement, but a dedicated order acceptance record is required before automated purchase launches.
- [ ] Give reasonable notice of material changes affecting active services; preserve accrued rights and prior accepted versions.
- [ ] Maintain project/retainer/hosting/email cancellation and handover procedures aligned with signed orders.

## Owner and solicitor sign-off

- [ ] Qualified UK solicitor reviews Master Service Terms, DPA, hosting/email terms, cancellations, liability, consumer boundaries and communications-service classification before contractual reliance.
- [ ] Owner confirms every commercial term and operational statement matches actual practice.
